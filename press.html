Short boilerplate (30 words)

StudioDrop turns phone snaps into studio-quality listing photos — removing backgrounds, adding a studio shadow and enhancing colour, entirely on your own iPhone, iPad or Mac. One purchase, no subscription.

Longer boilerplate (85 words)

StudioDrop is background removal for people who sell things online, built around one decision: do the work on the device instead of in the cloud. It removes the background from product photos, drops the item onto a clean backdrop with a soft studio shadow, enhances lighting and colour and crops to the shape your marketplace wants — for a whole shoot at once. Because it uses Apple's on-device machine learning, nothing is ever uploaded, there are no credits to run out, and it works with no signal. Buy once: £7.99 on iOS, £19.99 on Mac.

The one-line angle

Almost every background remover is a subscription to a cloud service — because they chose to process your photos on a server that costs them money. StudioDrop uses the neural engine already in your phone, so it can be sold once and never metered.

About the developer

StudioDrop is made by Connor Clements, an independent developer and online reseller in the United Kingdom, working alone. The app exists because every background remover he tried wanted a monthly fee for something his phone could already do. Available for interview, quotes and demos.

Correct usage

  • Written StudioDrop and StudioDrop Go — one word, capital S and D.
  • They are two separate apps and two separate purchases, not a single universal app.
  • Processing is on-device via Apple's Vision framework. Please don't describe it as a cloud or AI service in the sense of a remote API — that's the specific thing it isn't.
